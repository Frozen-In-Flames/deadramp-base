## Why

The second tranche of the Phase 4 brand research pass, continuing down the catalog: the remaining multi-offering vendors on the federal finish, then the single-offering vendors in registry order. Same rule as tranche 1, unchanged: a palette is seeded only when a citable public source establishes the vendor's colors as published hex values, verified against the source before seeding, and a vendor whose colors cannot be established keeps the federal finish.

## What is new

- 52 more vendors wear their own researched colors, 153 of 674 researched in total. Among them: the U.S. Department of the Treasury, 18F, the American Heart Association (from its own brand guidelines), Anthropic (from its own published brand file, accent verified against the source file directly), Asana, Acquia, Alation, Armis, Articulate, and the rest of the tranche listed in the exporter's seed table, each with its source recorded beside it.
- Two of tranche 1's holdouts resolved honestly: Casepoint's current listing now describes the right company and was verified before seeding, and Kiteworks' colors are now published as text rather than only inside a media-kit image.
- 8 vendors stayed default on the record, including two listings rejected for describing the wrong company entirely (one page's brand description was another product's, word for word) and one acquirer palette that could not be attributed to the acquired vendor. The rule held in both directions: evidence in, or the federal finish stays.
