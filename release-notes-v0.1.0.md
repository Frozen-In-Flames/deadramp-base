## What this is

DeadRamp Base is the walkable digital twin of the FedRAMP marketplace, generated entirely from the finished DeadRamp registry. One spiral boulevard, 81 districts, 674 vendor buildings, 781 offerings, every fact drawn from the public record the registry already verified. You walk it in first person and read the federal spend with your eyes: who is getting what, and who is doing what.

## Why

DeadRamp proved the data by rebuilding every offering clean-room. The Base exists so a human can SEE that proof. Text registries hide the shape of the marketplace; a city makes it legible at a glance, status as architecture, tier on the gate pylons, each vendor's identity in its colors.

## What is in Phase 0 and Phase 1

- A deterministic catalog exporter with 26 fidelity tests: every offering field byte-equal to the registry, the 2 unclassified offerings flagged honestly, exactly 1 empty-lot vendor, re-export byte-identical, and a mutation control proving one changed field changes exactly one offering's data.
- The spiral city exterior: one federal grammar (limestone, granite, bronze) modulated per building by the vendor's brand palette. 77 palettes are researched from established public brand identity; the other 597 wear the honest federal finish and are marked default, never invented. Pipeline vendors weather toward scaffold grey; certified buildings burn lit windows at dusk.
- Movement and records: WASD and mouse on desktop, twin-stick touch on mobile, proximity labels, and an info card per building carrying the complete public record with its Marketplace source link.
- The framing, on the kiosk, every card, and the page itself: clean-room model built from public FedRAMP Marketplace information, inspired by the vendor's public brand identity. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the vendor.

## Verification

Headless browser checks re-run green against the page's own reported numbers: 674 vendors rendered (673 buildings plus 1 lot), 781 offerings indexed, 81 districts, 18 draw calls and 48,242 triangles at spawn, zero JavaScript errors, movement proven by camera displacement, and pixel sampling confirms rich frames at spawn, in a district street, and in overview.
