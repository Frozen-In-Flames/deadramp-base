Accessibility and comfort
Keyboard controls

WASD or the arrow keys walk. The comma and period keys turn. E enters at a door and launches the terminal in a room. Enter opens the record of whatever you are looking at. Shift moves fast. Escape closes the topmost panel.

Accessibility statement

The Base is a three-dimensional scene, and everything in it is also available as text. The Directory lists every district, vendor, and offering with its status and launch mode, and can place you at any building or take you inside it. Arrivals, doors, floors, records, and terminal sessions are announced to assistive technology through a polite live region, and the terminal output is a live log. Known limits: the in-headset WebXR panels are visual only, because WebXR offers no assistive channel today; the on-device checks with VoiceOver and on the Quest 2 are still to be run; and signage inside the scene is carried by the Directory rather than by text inside the canvas.
