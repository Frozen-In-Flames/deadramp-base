## Why

Phase 4 opens with the brand layer. A building is recognizable as its vendor before you read the sign only if its colors are that vendor's real public identity, and the Base's integrity rule has never bent on this: a palette is seeded only when a citable public source establishes it, and every other vendor wears the neutral federal finish rather than an invented scheme. This release is the first research tranche of that pass, taking the largest remaining vendors first, the ones whose buildings anchor their districts.

## What is new

- 24 more vendors now wear their own researched colors, 101 of 674 researched in total: Project Hosts, stackArmor, Trellix, Unison, AvePoint, CGI Federal, Commvault, Exiger, Deltek (Replicon), DocuSign, Dynatrace, HP, Ivanti, Keeper Security, KnowBe4, MAXIMUS, NinjaOne, Nintex, Skillsoft, Slack, Trend Micro, Tyler Federal, the U.S. Department of Agriculture, and Wiz. Every seed records its source in the exporter: the vendor's own brand guidelines where they publish them (Commvault, DocuSign, Ivanti, NinjaOne, Nintex, Slack, Trend Micro, USDA), a trademark filing in Keeper Security's case, and established brand listings for the rest.
- 16 vendors in the same tranche stayed on the federal finish, on the record: their colors could not be established as published hex values from a citable source. One listing was rejected outright because it contradicted the identity it claimed to describe. Staying default is the rule working, not a gap.
- Nothing else changed: the catalog's facts, layout, and rooms are byte-for-byte what they were, proven by the fidelity suite below.

## Verification

Exporter fidelity tests pass in full: 781 offerings and 674 vendors, every offering field byte-equal to the registry, deterministic byte-identical re-export, both mutation negative controls exact, and the brand-integrity checks (no dead seeds, researched count equals the seed table, every default vendor wearing exactly the federal finish). The full site suite passes with zero JavaScript errors. Spot checks on the new seeds (DocuSign, Slack, Trellix, USDA) match their sources.
