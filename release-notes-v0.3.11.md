## Why

The eleventh and final tranche of the Phase 4 brand research pass. With this release, every one of the 674 vendors in the Base carries a researched decision: a palette seeded from a citable public source, verified against that source, or a recorded decision to keep the federal finish because no citable source establishes the vendor's colors.

## What is new

- 29 more vendors wear their own researched colors, bringing the pass to its final totals: 600 of 674 vendors researched, 74 staying in the federal finish on the record. This tranche includes Veracode, Verily, Verkada, Virtru, WalkMe, Workiva, Wolters Kluwer, Xerox, Zimperium, and the rest of the tranche in the exporter's seed table, each with its source recorded beside it.
- 4 vendors stayed default, including one whose listing presents its parent group's palette expressly as the parent's own, and two whose only listings state exactly two colors, where the third slot would have to be invented.
- The flagged judgment call in this tranche, a vendor whose listing carries generic boilerplate over its own domain and mission statement, was re-checked first-hand by the main side against the source page before shipping; the seed stands, with the tension recorded in its seed comment.
- Across the whole pass, the integrity rules held end to end: no palette was ever invented, no accent filled in, no listing used after it was found to describe the wrong company, an acquirer, or a successor, and the listing service's placeholder palette was rejected on sight wherever it appeared.

## Verification

Exporter fidelity tests pass in full (781 offerings, 674 vendors, byte-equal fields, deterministic re-export, both mutation controls, brand-integrity checks including researched count equals seed-table size). The full site suite passes with zero JavaScript errors. Main-side spot checks on the new seeds all match their sources, and the fallback-trio guard confirms zero researched vendors carry it.
