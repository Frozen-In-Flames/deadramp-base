## Why

The Base was always designed for this: the world hangs off one group, the visitor off a player record, precisely so a WebXR rig could replace the control layer without touching anything else. Phase 3 is that replacement. The Base is a place, and a place you can only visit through a flat window is half a place; standing inside the spiral city, at the scale of the buildings, is the experience the whole layout law was written for. This release takes the Base into the headset on the Quest 2 floor the performance budget was set against, through Quest Browser over HTTPS, with no app store and no sideload, the same standards-first route as every other platform the Base runs on.

## What is new

- Enter VR, feature-detected, never a dead button. The button appears only when the browser proves immersive-vr support. The session negotiates its reference space down a ladder (local-floor required, then local, then viewer), ending any half-made session cleanly before the next step, and the granted space is recorded. While presenting, the button reads Exit VR.
- The visitor is the camera. The camera now rides in a rig that carries the player record's position and yaw; the headset pose drives the view inside it, and physical head turns and room-scale steps layer onto the same position. Entering and leaving VR never moves the visitor: stand on floor 3 of a tower in 2D, enter VR, and you are standing on floor 3 in VR. Leaving restores the exact flat view, including its projection.
- Controller locomotion on the shared movement model. Left thumbstick walks, head-directed (forward is where you look); right thumbstick snap-turns in 45 degree steps, the comfort choice over smooth turning; a thumbstick click is the fast modifier. Speeds, bounds, collision, and the interior floor logic are the same code as every other device, so the spiral ramp climbs in VR exactly as it does on foot in 2D.
- Pointing in controller language. A ray line per controller; the trigger picks a building and opens its record on an in-world card (the DOM card is invisible in a headset), or activates the door you are standing at, with the same priority as a tap. A grip squeeze recalls the help, or closes an open record. Devices without controllers get a gaze ray with a centre reticle and a 1.2 second dwell.
- Platform-prompt rule, enforced in the headset. While presenting, every prompt speaks controller and gaze language only: "Trigger — Enter …", "Trigger — Exit to the boulevard". The input-mode resolver ranks XR above touch (a headset browser also reports touch points; the visitor is holding controllers, not a touchscreen). The help lifecycle carries over onto an in-world panel on the same schedule: shows, fades, and is recalled by squeeze.
- Comfort and performance. Fixed foveation is set to 0.6, a conservative default against the renderer's own maximum of 1.0, keeping peripheral signage readable while easing the Quest 2's fragment load. The XR layer allocates nothing per frame, panel canvases redraw only when their text changes, and the merged/instanced draw strategy is untouched. The main loop moved from requestAnimationFrame to renderer.setAnimationLoop, the one loop API that follows the visitor into a session and back; flat behaviour is identical.

## Verification

A new suite (verify/verify-xr.js) verifies everything up to the session boundary in headless Chrome: with WebXR absent, the page loads with zero JavaScript errors, the affordance agrees with feature detection, and the flat experience is intact; against a stubbed XRSystem that claims support but refuses sessions, the button appears and the full negotiation ladder runs in order and fails cleanly with the error recorded and the button restored. All three existing suites pass unchanged: the full Phase 1 and 2 suite, the walk-only climb suite (floor 3 reached on foot at 10.08 m), and the platform suite.

What headless verification cannot do is present. Headless Chrome has no XR runtime, so the session itself is verified on Aries's Quest 2, the standing Phase 3 gate. The on-device checklist: open the live page in Quest Browser and confirm the Enter VR button appears; enter VR at the plaza and confirm the view is at standing height where the flat view left off; walk with the left thumbstick and snap-turn with the right; enter a building by its door trigger and climb the spiral to an upper floor; point at a building, pull the trigger, and read the record card; confirm prompts and help speak controller language only; test the gaze dwell on a controller-less device if one is at hand; exit VR and confirm the flat view is exactly where it was; and walk the densest district watching frame rate. Until that pass is done, this release is verified to the session boundary, and no further.
