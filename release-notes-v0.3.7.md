## Why

The seventh tranche of the Phase 4 brand research pass, continuing in registry order under the same rule: a palette is seeded only when a citable public source establishes the vendor's colors as published hex values, verified against the source before seeding, and a vendor whose colors cannot be established keeps the federal finish.

## What is new

- 53 more vendors wear their own researched colors, 416 of 674 researched in total, including LogicMonitor, Lookout, Lucid Software, Magnet Forensics, Menlo Security, Moveworks, NetApp, Netskope, New Relic, Nexthink, and the rest of the tranche in the exporter's seed table, each with its source recorded beside it.
- Two vendors were seeded from their own published brand guidelines, which outrank listings: Medallia from its own brand page and Moodle from its own Global Brand Guidelines, in both cases rejecting listing palettes that match nothing the vendor itself publishes.
- 7 vendors stayed default on the record, including one major vendor whose only listing mixes its legacy palette with prose describing its successor identity (the company now trades under a new name that is not retained in its lockup, so neither palette is attributable), and three vendors that could not be identified to any citable source at all.
- One verified palette was deliberately held at the tranche boundary: a vendor researched and confirmed this pass sits one position past the tranche's count, so it was left unseeded on the record and will open the next tranche, keeping every tranche boundary exact.
