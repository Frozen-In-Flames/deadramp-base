## Why

The tenth tranche of the Phase 4 brand research pass, continuing in registry order under the same rule: a palette is seeded only when a citable public source establishes the vendor's colors as published hex values, verified against the source before seeding, and a vendor whose colors cannot be established keeps the federal finish.

## What is new

- 49 more vendors wear their own researched colors, 571 of 674 researched in total, including Sumo Logic, Synack, Talkdesk, Tanium, TeamViewer, Telos, Trimble, TRM Labs, Unqork, Vanta, and the rest of the tranche in the exporter's seed table, each with its source recorded beside it. The tranche opens with the capitalized StackArmor entry, confirmed against the same source as its lowercase duplicate from the first tranche.
- Trimble was seeded from Trimble's own Modus design system after its listing page was found to present a different company's palette under that company's name. Another vendor's wrong-company palette, a same-named rewards company, was fetched, identified, and set aside in favor of the correct company's own page.
- 11 vendors stayed default on the record, including two whose listings now present their acquirers' brands and palettes, one listing rejected because its header and its About describe two different companies with the same name, and one vendor for whom only a single logo color is citable anywhere.
- One flagged judgment call was re-checked first-hand by the main side before shipping: a vendor whose listing carries stale legacy copy about the same legal entity at its own domain, with the palette stated for that entity. The seed stands, with the staleness recorded in its seed comment.
