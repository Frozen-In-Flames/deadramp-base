## Why

Phase 4 makes the Base read as what it is: the whole marketplace, with its structure visible. Three parts of DeadRamp's record were true but invisible in the city until now: which builds descend from which, which capabilities were decided once as a shared platform, and which authorizations are still in progress. This release builds all three into the architecture.

## What is new

- Skybridges for the merges that exist. DeadRamp's records contain no vendor to vendor merges, and none were invented. The merge record is build lineage: 18 builds whose UNION-SPEC lineage sections name 19 parent edges. Each edge now spans as a skybridge between the district gates where the child and parent builds' offerings actually live. Picking a bridge opens its record: the two builds, the merge kind, the Merge Rule, and the spec path. A walkable bridge interior was not built; the record card is the exhibit, stated plainly.
- The platform deck. One shared deck on the plaza carries the six platform layer decisions from PLATFORM-LAYER.md, with their control citations, and the directory of the 40 re-scoped entries counted by section from GAPS.md. One accounting note, kept visible in LAYOUT.md: the gaps batch decision document accounts for 39 re-scope closures while GAPS.md carries 40 entry lines; the deck presents the 40 in GAPS.md, the fuller record.
- Construction states. Every building with offerings still in the pipeline now shows it: scaffolding rises in proportion to the share of its own offerings not yet certified, fully pending towers wear netting with barriers and signage, and 17 cranes stand under a stated exporter rule (the tallest fully pending building in each district with at least three pipeline buildings; crane placement is construction vocabulary assigned by that rule, not a fact in any vendor's record). Certified buildings and the two unclassified lots are untouched.

## Verification

Exporter fidelity tests pass in full, including two new negative controls: removing the lineage and platform sources yields zero merges and zero platform entries, and flipping one vendor's status changes exactly one construction record. All five site suites pass with zero JavaScript errors: the full suite (bridges 19, construction buildings 227, cranes 17, both new record picks correct), the walk suite, the platform prompt suite, and the XR boundary suite. Spawn view draw calls moved from 18 to 22; the new geometry is instanced throughout.
